🧼 Food Safety & Hygiene

  • Always wash hands with soap before handling food.
  • Use separate cutting boards for raw meat and vegetables.
  • Keep your stall and equipment clean at all times.
  • Store food at correct temperatures to prevent spoilage.

📜 Licensing & Permits

  • Ensure you have a valid FSSAI registration/license.
  • Display your license clearly at your stall.
  • Check with your local municipality for any additional permits required.
  • Keep digital and physical copies of all your documents.
